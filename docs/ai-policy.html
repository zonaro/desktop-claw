AI policy

This policy explains how AI tools (like large language models) may be used when contributing to Desktop Claw.

Code

AI-generated code is allowed. However, the author takes full responsibility for its correctness. Before opening a pull request, the author must read all the code, understand it, and change whatever needs changing. Treat AI output as a draft you are accountable for, not as finished work.

Communication

All interaction in issues and pull requests, including descriptions and comments, must be written by a human. Two exceptions are allowed:

  • Translation. You can use AI to translate your message into another language (usually English). The ideas and text must still be your own, AI is only translating them.
  • Quotes and technical analysis. You may append a quote or an analysis produced by an LLM, as long as it is clearly marked. A reader must be able to tell, without any doubt, which parts are written by you and which parts come from an AI.
